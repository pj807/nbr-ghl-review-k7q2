Account email settings

Rules for every send

  • Sender Domain: mail.nbrtribe.com. Never "All Domains" or "rotate domains".
  • From address ends in @mail.nbrtribe.com.
  • Send only to people who opened or clicked recently, one brand at a time (ISLA, NBR or BMS). Never the full list, and never contacts with no opens in 180+ days.
  • Include an unsubscribe link.
  • Send a test to a Gmail address first and check SPF, DKIM and DMARC pass.
  • Keep sending steadily. Long gaps followed by big sends hurt the domain's reputation.

Good to know

  • Social Planner, Countdown Timers, Payment Links and Conversations weren't checked (the pages didn't load in time). Tools outside GHL weren't checked either.
  • Check the exact times before acting on them: some times in the audit were labeled a day ahead of EST.
  • Rules come from Greg Swafford's Email & Domains guide (Nov 18). The full cleanup review is on the Cleanup review page.
